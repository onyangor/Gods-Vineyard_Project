PHOTOS OF FOUNDERS AND LEADERS
1. Save each photo here, e.g. founder.jpg, cofounder.jpg, keynote-speaker.jpg (portrait photos work best, under 300 KB).
2. Open src/data/about.js and add a line to that person:  photo: "/people/founder.jpg"
3. Photos are shown whole, never cropped. Without a photo, a round placeholder is shown.
